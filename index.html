How Express PLUS works
  • One skip at each haunted house. Four houses, four skips. Use them in the first two hours.
  • One skip at each participating ride. Mummy, Battlestar, Transformers and the rest.
  • Dated to 29 Oct. Not transferable to another night.
  • Admission sold separately. You need both QR codes on your phone at the gate.

Good to know

Small things.

Costumes are allowed.No full face masks

Face paint only up to half the face. No props that look like weapons, no uniforms, nothing that could be mistaken for park cast.

Roaming horror.

The Karang Guni man stalks the pathways looking for new pieces for his collection. The Fatimah Rockers, a pontianak dance troupe, strike without warning outside The RAVEyard.

Shoes you can walk in.

Four houses, two zones, three rides and a lot of pavement between them. Wear trainers.

Phones on low power.

Tickets, Express passes and Grab home all live on your phone. Charge before you leave.
